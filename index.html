☸️ The Wheel of Dharma

The Wheel of Dharma represents the order that keeps life in balance.

Every spoke reminds us that actions have consequences. When actions are guided by truth, restraint, and compassion, life moves smoothly.

When these are ignored, imbalance appears — first within the individual, and then within society.

Dharma does not force direction. It quietly shows the way forward.

👪 Filial Piety

Filial Piety begins with remembering those who made our life possible.

Parents give without keeping accounts — time, strength, patience, and silent sacrifice.

Honoring parents is not about control or tradition. It is about gratitude, responsibility, and continuity.

When respect starts at home, character forms naturally.

Those who honor their parents rarely lose their way.

🤝 Brotherhood

Brotherhood is the understanding that we do not live alone.

Every person carries unseen struggles, hopes, and fears — much like our own.

When we act only for ourselves, distance grows. When we act with consideration, trust forms.

A society survives not by strength alone, but by how its people treat one another.

Strength grows when compassion is shared.

🌿 A Moment of Reflection

A wheel once stood at the center of a village path.

An elder maintained it, saying, “As long as this wheel turns smoothly, the path remains clear.”

One day, a young man asked, “What keeps the wheel turning?”

The elder replied, “Respect at home gives it strength, kindness to others gives it direction, and righteous action keeps it in balance.”

The wheel was never worshipped — yet the village walked its path without losing the way.

“Dharma begins at home, and extends to the world.”
